The Classmate Matrix

Your self-rankings are a feature vector. The class roster is a stack of keys. One dot product per pair, softmax per row — and the class becomes an attention matrix.

Everything typed here stays in this browser tab — nothing is uploaded or saved. A nickname or invented persona works exactly as well as a real one.

Step 1The roster — one stat card per student

Rank each quality 0–10. Before scoring, every ranking is divided by 10 so features live between 0 and 1 — that keeps the dot products in a range softmax can spread sensibly, whatever d is.

paste a roster (CSV or tab-separated)

First line may be a header starting with name followed by the quality names. Every other line: name, 4, 7, 2, …

Step 2The class attention matrix

Cell (row i, column j) is how much of student i's attention lands on student j: score = (q · k) / √d over the normalized features, then softmax across each row. Darker cell, louder volume. Every row sums to 1. Cell shade is scaled within each row so the loudest classmate stands out — the printed number is the true weight. Hover a cell for the raw score.

Step 3Spotlight one query

A dark cell between two students is a statement about a handful of self-reported numbers — not about friendship, compatibility, or worth. That is the same reason a large attention weight is never a complete explanation of a model's decision.
