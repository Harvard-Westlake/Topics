Step 1Score every permitted position

Step 2Softmax turns scores into weights

Subtract the maximum, exponentiate, divide by the sum. Scores can be any real number; weights are positive and sum to exactly 1.

Step 3The attention row — one volume dial per card

Step 4Blend the values

Each card's stat card is multiplied by its weight and added up, slot by slot. The grey bar is Stage 1's equal mixing at this same position — the blur the spotlight must beat.

Cards after the current position are masked: they are never scored, and their weight is recorded as exactly 0.000 — the only exact zero on this page. Swap a masked card for the odd one out (GYM SOCK, LEAF BLOWER) and confirm that nothing above changes.
