# The Ethics and Context Strand
*<font color="#8b949e">One authentic social-context question per chapter, grounded in your own corpus</font>*

</div>

---

Social context is not confined to one final lecture. Each technical chapter includes a small but authentic question:

| Chapter | Context question |
|---|---|
| 1 | Who selected the corpus, and who had permission to distribute it? |
| 2 | Whose language becomes statistically "normal" in this corpus? |
| 3 | Is an attention weight an adequate explanation? |
| 4 | Does optimizing frequency reward truth, popularity, or merely repetition? |
| 5 | What relationships might embeddings encode unintentionally? |
| 6 | Who is excluded when only mathematically sophisticated explanations are treated as rigorous? |
| 7 | How do hardware and runtime constraints affect who can participate? |
| 8 | Can an attention visualization create a false sense of interpretability? |
| 9 | Does a larger model necessarily represent a better educational result? |
| 10 | When does generation become memorization or reproduction? |
| 11 | How might generated text misrepresent a community, author, or scientific source? |

This strand remains evidence-based and connected to your actual corpus. It should not become a detached weekly discussion unrelated to your code.
