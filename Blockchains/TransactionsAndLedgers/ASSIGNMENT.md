*Lesson: [Transactions and Ledgers](README.md)*

---

Explore the real Bitcoin blockchain and choose a block mined **before block 758142**. Use a public block explorer such as [mempool.space](https://mempool.space) or [blockchain.com's explorer](https://www.blockchain.com/explorer) — type a block number into the search bar and start reading.

## <font color="#388bfd">B-grade tier</font>

Answer the following about your chosen block:

1. How difficult was the block to mine — in terms of how many 0's are at the front of its hash?
2. How many transactions are in this block?
3. How much was the block reward, in BTC?
4. What is the transaction ID of the largest transaction in this block?

## <font color="#388bfd">Honors A-tier</font>

Pick a block **before block 700000 with more than 200 transactions**, then also answer:

1. What date was it mined, and how much was the reward worth in USD at the time of mining?
2. What is the transaction ID of the largest transaction *apart from* the block reward?
3. For that transaction, how many other transactions has that wallet had?

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **Valid block chosen** — your block number is below 758142 (below 700000 with 200+ transactions for the A-tier)
- [ ] **Difficulty read from the hash** — you counted the leading zeros on the block hash itself, not a difficulty statistic
- [ ] **Reward identified** — you found the block reward transaction and its BTC amount
- [ ] **Largest transaction found** — you sorted or scanned the block's transactions and recorded a full transaction ID
- [ ] **A-tier only: wallet history traced** — you followed the largest non-reward transaction back to its wallet and counted its other transactions
