# Assignment — Crypto Research Project

*Lesson: [Crypto Research Project](README.md)*

**Due:** Presentation day, set in class
**Points:** 200

---

Research and present a cryptocurrency or blockchain technology from the **top 100 on [coinmarketcap.com](https://coinmarketcap.com/)**, answering **Who, What, How, Where, When, and Why**.

## Requirements

- **Topic selection:** One cryptocurrency or blockchain technology in the top 100 on coinmarketcap.com.
- **Content:**
  - **Who:** Background on the creator(s) and origin story.
  - **What:** What the technology is and how it works.
  - **How:** A code snippet key to its functionality and novelty (for open-source projects). Its tokenomics — how the token is minted, distributed, allocated — blockchain values, technical features, token distribution, and what makes it unique.
  - **Where & When:** Where and when it was developed and implemented.
  - **Why:** The purpose behind its creation and its significance.
  - **Additional:** Comparisons to similar technologies and a brief look at future prospects or technical challenges.
- **Presentation guidelines:**
  - Duration: **4–6 minutes**
  - Slides with minimal text and clear visuals
  - Engage your audience by speaking confidently — do not simply read your slides
  - Show respect to your peers during their presentations
- **Submission:** Submit a copy of your slides along with a list of sources by the due date.
