# Assignment — Smart Chains and Wrapped Bitcoin

*Lesson: [Smart Chains and Wrapped Bitcoin](README.md)*

**Due:** Next class
**Points:** 50

---

Complete the in-class activity end to end: enable the Sepolia test network in your wallet, fund it from the faucet, and send testnet ETH to a classmate. Then find your **first transaction** on a block explorer.

## Steps

1. Enable test networks in your wallet (Phantom: Settings → Developer Settings → **Enable Test Networks**).
2. Get Sepolia test ETH: [https://cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia](https://cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia)
3. Send some testnet ETH to a classmate's address — sending from your wallet proves you own it.
4. Look your address up on [sepolia.etherscan.io](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/), open your first transaction, and record the **block number** it was included in.

> **Warning:**
> Testnets and play-money only. Never put real money into a class wallet, and never share a private key or seed phrase with anyone, including your teacher. Your **address** is public and safe to submit — that is the whole point of an address.

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **Test network enabled** — your wallet shows Sepolia and a Sepolia balance
- [ ] **Faucet funds received** — testnet ETH from the faucet appears in your wallet
- [ ] **Transaction sent** — you sent testnet ETH from your wallet to a classmate's address
- [ ] **Block identified** — you found your first transaction on sepolia.etherscan.io and recorded its block number
- [ ] **Nothing secret shared** — you submitted only your public address; no seed phrase or private key appears anywhere
