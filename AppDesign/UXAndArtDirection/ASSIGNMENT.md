# Assignment — UX and Art Direction

*Lesson: [UX and Art Direction](README.md)*

**Due:** Next class

---

In a mixed team of UX Design and Art Direction students, develop a concept for an app or game that incorporates both disciplines. Each member produces the artifacts of their chosen track; the team integrates them into one cohesive project.

## Requirements (depending on your path)

1. **UX path** — a UX flowchart and a wireframe for a key part of the app/game, plus a plan for how and when user testing and feedback will be incorporated
2. **Art path** — a mood board and several art assets that match the project's theme (palette via [Coolors](https://coolors.co/), sprites via [Piskel](https://www.piskelapp.com/) welcome)

## Evaluation criteria (30 points)

- Creativity in integrating UX and Art Direction elements
- Clarity and functionality of the UX design
- Aesthetic appeal and thematic consistency of the art direction
- Is this more than 2 hours of work?

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **Team is mixed** — it contains at least one UX Design student and one Art Direction student
- [ ] **UX artifacts complete** — the flowchart covers the key user journey and the wireframe covers a key screen or interaction
- [ ] **Art artifacts complete** — a mood board plus several assets, all sharing one visual theme
- [ ] **The two integrate** — the wireframed screens and the art direction describe the same product, not two products
- [ ] **Effort is visible** — the combined work clearly exceeds two hours
