# Design Refinement
*<font color="#8b949e">Walk every idea in small groups until the design passes the detail test</font>*

<font color="#a371f7">Reinforce</font>

</div>

---

For however long it takes — usually 2 to 3 class days — we group up into **groups of 4–5** and review **every idea available** for each design. Nothing gets skipped: every contribution from every classmate gets discussed, and either woven into the design in full detail or deliberately removed.

> **Note:**
> If you have trouble figuring out which group you are in, the amount you bid determines priority — you refine the designs you backed the most.

---

## <font color="#388bfd">The process</font>

1. **Share a working document** with your small group so everyone can edit and comment together
2. **Go through each design contribution** from your classmates, one at a time:
   - **Discuss / add / remove** — is this a desirable user feature or experience? Improve it, extend it, or cut it
   - **Tie it into the overall design** and explain it in as much detail as possible

Every decision is a design decision: made in terms of user value and experience, never in terms of how hard it would be to program.

---

## <font color="#388bfd">The detail test</font>

How do you know a section is detailed enough?

> **Tip:**
> **If two people NOT in your group had to implement the design, is it clear?** If two strangers would build two different features from your words, the section is not done. Keep elaborating until any reader would build the same thing.

This test is the heartbeat of the next three lessons too — it is exactly the standard a technical specification will demand, applied here to pure design language.

---

## <font color="#388bfd">Pacing across the days</font>

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| First day | Walk the highest-priority ideas; establish the group's rhythm of discuss / add / remove |
| Second day | Continue through every remaining contribution; start tying refined ideas back into the overall design document |
| Third day (if needed) | Close the gaps — run the detail test section by section and elaborate anything that fails |

Get the refined sections back into the design repository as you go — the shared working document is scaffolding, the repository is the record.

---

## <font color="#388bfd">☑️ Check for Understanding</font>

### <font color="#79c0ff">Introductory</font>

- [ ] State the detail test from memory.
- [ ] Name the three verbs your group applies to every contribution (discuss, add, remove).
- [ ] Explain why every idea gets reviewed, not just the popular ones.

### <font color="#79c0ff">Intermediate</font>

- [ ] Take a one-line feature idea and expand it until it passes the detail test.
- [ ] Explain what "tie it into the overall design" means, with an example of a feature that conflicts until tied in.
- [ ] Make the case for removing a feature using only user-value arguments.

### <font color="#79c0ff">Advanced</font>

- [ ] Referee a group disagreement about a feature using the design-communication rules.
- [ ] Identify a section that *seems* detailed but would still produce two different implementations.
- [ ] Keep a three-day refinement organized so no contribution is silently dropped.
