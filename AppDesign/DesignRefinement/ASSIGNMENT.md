# Assignment — Design Refinement

**Duration:** 3 class periods  
**Due:** End of the final refinement day

---

In groups of 4–5, review every design contribution from your classmates: discuss, add, or remove each one as a user feature or experience, tie it into the overall design, and explain it in as much detail as possible.

## Steps

1. Form your group of 4–5 (bid amounts determine priority if group membership is unclear)
2. Share a working document with your group
3. Walk **every** design contribution, one at a time:
   - Decide: keep (and elaborate), or remove — argued entirely in terms of user value and experience
   - Tie each kept idea into the overall design
4. Apply the detail test to every refined section: **if two people NOT in your group had to implement the design, is it clear?**
5. Commit the refined sections back into the design repository

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **Every contribution reviewed** — no classmate's idea was skipped; each was kept-and-detailed or deliberately removed
- [ ] **Decisions argued as design** — reasons are about user value and experience, never programming difficulty
- [ ] **Refined sections pass the detail test** — two people outside your group would build the same feature from your words
- [ ] **Design repository updated** — the refined sections are committed, not stranded in the working document
- [ ] **Group worked as a group** — every member can explain any refined section
