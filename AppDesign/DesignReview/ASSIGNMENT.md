# Assignment — Design Review

**Due:** Next class

---

Review your partner's design in a branch, open a pull request with your review, then integrate the review you received into your own design and reformat it as Markdown. This assignment is worth **20 pts**.

## Steps

1. Read your partner's design (10 minutes), then watch their pitch video
2. Get added as a **collaborator** on their repository and accept the invite
3. Create a **branch other than `main`** for your review work
4. Add your review directly to the design document: unclear passages, missing behaviors, implementation drift, Honor Code or legal concerns, and constructive ideas (praise only where earned)
5. Commit your review and open a **pull request**
6. Homework: integrate the review comments you received into your own design, and **reformat your design to Markdown** — the end design must still be a comprehensive explanation of your idea and how it works in detail

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **Collaborator access confirmed** — you accepted the invite and can push branches to your partner's repo
- [ ] **Review in a branch** — none of your review commits touch `main`
- [ ] **Review stays on design** — comments address ideas, value, and user experience, never how to program anything
- [ ] **Honor Code and law check done** — you looked for and flagged (or cleared) violations
- [ ] **Pull request open** — your branch with the in-class review is submitted as a PR on your partner's repo
- [ ] **Peer feedback integrated** — your own design has a commit incorporating your partner's review
- [ ] **Design is Markdown** — your design document uses real Markdown structure (headings, lists, tables)
