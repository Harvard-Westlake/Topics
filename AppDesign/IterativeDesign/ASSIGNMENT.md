# Assignment — Iterative Design

**Due:** Next class

---

Iterate on the chosen design: define what "better" means, plan your improvements, and commit them to a branch. This assignment is worth **15 pts**.

## Steps

1. Read and examine **all** existing information in the chosen design repository
2. Write your definition of "better" (focused on interactive design) and your improvement blueprint — why and how each enhancement uplifts the design
3. Clone the repository and become a collaborator
4. Create a branch to work in
5. Make **at least 3 commits, each one hour apart, each with significant changes** rich in detail
6. Push your branch to GitHub and prepare your presentation — remember, you may not read off your notes (that is half your presentation points; pictures are ok)

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **"Better" defined** — your definition is written into the design work, in your own words
- [ ] **Blueprint explains why and how** — every enhancement has a reason, not just a description
- [ ] **Branch pushed** — your work is in its own branch on GitHub, not on `main`
- [ ] **Three significant commits, an hour apart** — timestamps prove it; each commit meaningfully advances the design
- [ ] **Presentation ready** — you can speak your ideas without reading your writing
