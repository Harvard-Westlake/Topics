# Assignment — Learning to Design

*Lesson: [Learning to Design](README.md)*

**Due:** End of class

---

This assignment has two graded parts: the game design writeup (**5 pts**) and the design-vs-specification evaluation (**10 pts**).

> **Warning:**
> No ChatGPT or other AI tools today. The work must be in your own words.

## Part 1 — Game Design Writeup (5 pts)

Write a design document for **your partner's** game containing all six sections: Game Concept, Main Characters, Game Environment, Behaviors, Functions, and Relationships. Use only non-technical language.

## Part 2 — Evaluate a Design Document (10 pts)

After trading documents with your partner:

1. **Categorized list** — sort the sections and points of your partner's document into "Game Design" and "Technical Specifications." If your partner wrote nothing technical, write technical specifications for 5 methods found in your own game's code instead.
2. **Feedback paragraph** — how well did your partner separate design from technical specification? Where were the two blended?
3. **Missing behavior** — name at least one design behavior or feature your partner's document missed.
4. **Reflection** — a few sentences on what was challenging about separating design from specification and how it will influence your future design work.

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **All six writeup sections present** — Concept, Characters, Environment, Behaviors, Functions, Relationships each have real content
- [ ] **Non-technical language throughout** — a non-programmer could picture the game from your writeup
- [ ] **Categorized list complete** — every section of your partner's document is sorted into Design or Technical Specifications
- [ ] **Feedback paragraph written** — it names specific places where design and specification were separated well or blended
- [ ] **Missing behavior identified** — at least one behavior or feature your partner overlooked is named
- [ ] **No AI use** — every word is your own
