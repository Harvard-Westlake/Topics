# The Design Challenge
*<font color="#8b949e">Design a large-scale app or game every student in the school could use</font>*

<font color="#a371f7">Reinforce</font>

</div>

---

This challenge asks you to conceptualize, design, and plan the development of a **large-scale web or mobile application or game**. It should be engaging, competitive, and usable by all students within the school, providing explicit value and utility. The design must also be **scalable** — built to grow beyond this assignment.

> **Note:**
> You will present this design to your classmates. **One of these designs WILL BE CHOSEN as the class project** — the product the whole class designs, specifies, and eventually builds together in [Group Programming](../../GroupProgramming/). Design like it might be yours.

---

## <font color="#388bfd">Business requirements</font>

Your design must satisfy all five:

| # | Requirement | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Universal Accessibility** | The application/game must be usable by all students at the school, ensuring inclusivity and adherence to the California non-discrimination clause |
| 2 | **Explicit Value/Use** | You must clearly articulate the specific value or use the application/game provides to its users |
| 3 | **Competitive Element** | The application/game should facilitate competition among students, encouraging engagement and participation |
| 4 | **Engagement and Addictiveness** | The experience should be more engaging and addictive than applications/games designed by other classes — measured by a combination of unique active users and median active user time spent on the app |
| 5 | **Scalability and Expansion** | The design must incorporate features or strategies allowing growth beyond the initial assignment — adoption by other schools, communities, or broader audiences, local and global |

---

## <font color="#388bfd">Legal and ethical considerations</font>

- **Non-Discrimination:** The design and implementation must follow the [California non-discrimination clause](https://tinyurl.com/canondescriminationclause). Ensure your application/game is accessible and inclusive to all potential users.

---

## <font color="#388bfd">What you will produce</font>

A **public GitHub repository** whose `README.md` documents the design completely — introduction, value, user experience, competitive element, engagement strategies, scalability, and accessibility. The full section list, point breakdown, and commit requirements are in the [assignment](ASSIGNMENT.md).

Your commit history matters as much as the document: at least **three commits, each at least an hour apart**, each representing a significant advancement in the design. Thoughtful, incremental development — not a last-minute dump.

> **Tip:**
> One last note: the sky is the limit. Coming up with a great idea sometimes means stretching what is thought possible.

## <font color="#388bfd">☑️ Check for Understanding</font>

### <font color="#79c0ff">Introductory</font>

- [ ] Name all five business requirements from memory.
- [ ] State your design's explicit value in one sentence.
- [ ] Explain what makes a repository "public" and why this assignment requires it.

### <font color="#79c0ff">Intermediate</font>

- [ ] Describe how your design's competitive element drives engagement between students.
- [ ] Explain how engagement will be measured (unique active users and median active time) and how your design scores well on both.
- [ ] Explain why the commit-an-hour-apart rule exists, in terms of how good designs develop.

### <font color="#79c0ff">Advanced</font>

- [ ] Walk through how your design satisfies the California non-discrimination clause, feature by feature.
- [ ] Describe a concrete expansion path for your design beyond this school.
- [ ] Defend your design against the question "why would a student open this a second time?"
