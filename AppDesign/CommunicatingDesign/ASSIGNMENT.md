# Assignment — Communicating Design

**Due:** Next class

---

Design a product — an app, program, or game — and prepare to sell it. Everyone in the class will see your presentation; one goal, no apologies: **make your idea stand out**.

## Deliverables

Create a **new GitHub repository** for your design and include:

1. **All your design materials** in the repository
2. A **1-page PDF summary** of your idea — its concept, its user interface, and how you plan to communicate it
3. Your **slide deck**, uploaded into the repository as a PowerPoint file (**-1 letter grade for a web-hosted link instead of an uploaded file**)
4. A **5-minute video presentation selling your idea**, walking through your slide deck — submitted on Canvas

Your repository's `README.md` must contain your full **product outline**, graded on the 100-point breakdown below.

> **Warning:**
> Any form of Google Doc submission gets a maximum of 50%.

---

## Product outline breakdown (100 pts)

| # | Section | Points | What it must contain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Problem Definition and User Research** | 25 | Does the product meet the qualifications for good ideas from our brainstorm? Identify the target audience and why the product is engaging / useful. Optionally add user personas or scenarios (for apps) |
| 2 | **Conceptualization and Design** | 30 | List the main features or functionalities; include sketches or details; explain the user flow or interaction model (app) or progression (game) |

## Design rubric

Outline examples below are written for games — repurpose freely for apps.

| Criteria | Needs Improvement (1–2) | Meets Expectations (3–4) | Exceeds Expectations (5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Creativity & Originality** | Concept feels generic, derivative, or unclear. Limited exploration of theme/variation, with minimal evidence of inspiration influencing the design | Concept is solid and appropriate for the target audience. Some original elements are present, and inspiration sources meaningfully inform the direction | Concept is highly original, memorable, and clearly differentiated. Inspiration is synthesized into a fresh direction with thoughtful variations explored before selecting a final approach |
| **Clarity of Design (Purpose + Look)** | Purpose and audience are vague or inconsistent with the design. Visual style feels unplanned (inconsistent theme, color, typography, layout), reducing readability and usability | Purpose and target audience are clearly defined and reflected in the design. Visual system (theme, palette, typography, layout) is mostly consistent and supports usability | Purpose and audience are exceptionally clear and guide every design decision. Visual identity is cohesive, polished, and intentional. It is clear in its comprehension, navigation, and overall product credibility |
| **Engaging Experience (Function + Interaction)** | Core features/mechanics are incomplete or confusing. User flow is fragmented, and interactions/controls are not clearly described or don't support the intended experience | Core features/mechanics are well-defined and align with the goal. User flow is logical, and interactive elements (buttons/menus/forms/controls) are clearly explained | Experience is notably engaging and user-centered. Features and interactions are thoughtfully designed to reduce friction and increase delight; user flow is seamless and anticipates user needs and edge cases |
| **Design Thoroughness & Communication (Artifacts + Submission)** | Wireframes/mockups are missing, too few, or do not represent key screens/states. 1-page summary and/or pitch deck lacks clarity or completeness. GitHub repo is disorganized or missing required materials | Wireframes cover key screens/pages; mockups show the intended final look with reasonable detail. Summary and pitch deck communicate the concept and main design choices. GitHub repo includes required items and is reasonably organized | Wireframes and mockups are comprehensive (key screens, states, and flows), with clear rationale for major decisions. Summary and pitch deck tell a compelling story and clearly explain how aesthetics + functionality work together. GitHub repo is clean, easy to navigate, and presentation-ready (clear structure, naming, and completeness) |

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **Repository created** — a new GitHub repo holds all your design materials
- [ ] **Outline complete** — the README covers all five outline sections with real content
- [ ] **1-page PDF present** — the summary is in the repo and actually fits on one page
- [ ] **Slide deck uploaded** — the PowerPoint file itself is in the repo, not a web link
- [ ] **Video recorded** — a 5-minute presentation selling your idea, walking through your slides
- [ ] **No implementation talk** — the design describes user value, behavior, and experience only
- [ ] **Every element intentional** — you can explain why each part of the design exists
