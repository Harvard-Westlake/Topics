# Assignment — Merging Designs

**Due:** End of class

---

Two graded parts: your individual additions to the chosen class design (**Part 1 — 100 pts**), and the pairwise merge of your work with a partner's (**Part 2 — 15 pts**).

## Part 1 — Build out the chosen design (100 pts)

1. Add yourself as a **collaborator** on the chosen project
2. **Clone** the repository — **do NOT fork**
3. Create a **branch** for your design work
4. Add **2 meaningful features**
5. Add **2 less important modifications** to the chosen design

## Part 2 — Pairwise merge (15 pts)

1. Pair with your randomly assigned classmate and read each other's additions
2. Work through every major idea with the **P1/P2 protocol** (explain; add an unconsidered benefit; raise an overlooked critical issue; switch roles) and give quick two-sided first impressions of minor ideas
3. Create a **branch for your joint work**
4. Merge your two branches into it, keeping **only your revised ideas**
5. Push the joint branch

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **Collaborator, not forker** — your work lives in the class repository, in your own branch
- [ ] **2 + 2 additions committed** — two meaningful features and two lesser modifications, all in design language
- [ ] **Protocol actually followed** — every major idea got a benefit the designer missed and a critical issue, from both sides
- [ ] **Joint branch pushed** — it contains the merged, revised ideas from both partners, not a raw concatenation
- [ ] **Communication stayed on the design** — no sentence took the designer as its subject
