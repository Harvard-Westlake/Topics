# Assignment — Team Design and Pitch

*Lesson: [Team Design and Pitch](README.md)*

**Due:** Next class (final presentation day)

---

Present your design to your team, integrate the team's best ideas into one cohesive design in the original designer's repository, and prepare a team presentation of the entire design. This assignment is worth **10 pts**.

## Steps

1. Present your design to your team; answer only questions about your design
2. Take notes during teammates' presentations on innovative or effective aspects
3. Discuss as a team: altered behaviors, new behaviors, and feature additions — no implementation talk
4. Integrate the best features from every member into a single, cohesive design
5. Commit the integrated design to the **original designer's repository** with clear commit messages
6. Prepare the team presentation and allocate a role to every member; finalize it for homework

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **You presented** — and answered questions without drifting into technical implementation
- [ ] **Notes taken** — you recorded specific effective aspects of at least two teammates' designs
- [ ] **Integrated design committed** — the original designer's repo contains the team's single cohesive design
- [ ] **Commit messages are clear** — each integration commit says which ideas were incorporated
- [ ] **Presentation prepared** — the team presentation covers the entire design and every member has a role
