# Assignment — Scrum and Stand-ups

*Lesson: [Scrum and Stand-ups](README.md)*

**Due:** End of class

---

Objectives by end of day, in class — worth **10 pts** today:

1. Have **all documentation and details in the GitHub repository**
2. Have everyone **check out the repository and sync** with the latest material
3. **Work on a task and submit it** in your repo — take your assigned design element and detail it in the style of the worked examples (drop rates, named categories, explicit rules)

Starting next class, every session opens with a timed **stand-up** (1–2 minutes per person), and assignments are graded as:

| Component | Points |
|---|---|
| SCRUM update | 8 |
| Work done during class | 12 |

Your stand-up update must make clear **what you worked on**, communicate it **clearly and specifically**, and **call out any items where you need help** from other people.

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **Repository synced** — you pulled the latest material and your local copy matches the repo
- [ ] **All documentation in GitHub** — nothing about your design element lives only in a doc or your head
- [ ] **Task detailed and committed** — your assigned element went from vague to detailed, with explicit rules a stranger could build from
- [ ] **Stand-up prepared** — you can deliver your 1–2 minute update: what you worked on, stated clearly, blockers called out
