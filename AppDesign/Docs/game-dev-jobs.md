# Game Dev Jobs
*<font color="#8b949e">The roles on a real development team</font>*

</div>

---

Shipping an app or game takes far more than programmers. This catalog lists the roles found on real development teams — useful when your class divides into specializations.

## <font color="#388bfd">Production</font>

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Producer / Project Manager** | Oversees the development process; manages timelines and budgets; coordinates between teams and stakeholders |

## <font color="#388bfd">Technical Jobs</font>

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Game Design Director** | Owns the overall vision and design of the game — gameplay mechanics, story, level design, and user experience |
| **Game Programmer** | Codes the game and implements the design; simulates physical systems like movement, collisions, and environmental interactions; develops AI for non-player characters; specializes in online aspects such as multiplayer systems, server management, and network security |
| **Quality Assurance Tester (QA)** | Tests the game for bugs, glitches, and other issues to ensure a smooth, enjoyable player experience — on a small team, this is everyone |
| **Technical Artist** | Bridges artists and programmers — shaders, special effects, and optimizing art assets for performance |

## <font color="#388bfd">Design Jobs</font>

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Art Director** | Owns the overall vision of the game's artistic expression — animations, music, background, and character design |
| **Level Designer** | Creates the levels or stages of the game — layout, challenge design, and player progression |
| **Story Director** | Owns the story, characters, dialogue, and world-building |
| **Graphic Artist / Game Artist** | Creates the visual elements — characters, environments, and UI components; crafts immersive, believable settings and landscapes |
| **Animator** | Brings characters and environments to life with motion — movements, expressions, and interactions |
| **Texture Artist** | Creates textures for models, such as character skins |
| **Sound Designer / Audio Engineer** | Owns the game's audio — sound effects, music, and voiceovers |
| **UI/UX Designer** | Designs the user interface and user experience so the game is intuitive and accessible; oversees the game's accessibility functions |

## <font color="#388bfd">Other Jobs</font>

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Community Manager** | Manages the game's community — social media, forums, player feedback — and acts as liaison between players and developers |
| **Localization Specialist** | Adapts the game for different languages and regions, ensuring cultural appropriateness and linguistic accuracy |
