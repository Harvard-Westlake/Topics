## The Rubric

Use each app for at least three minutes, for what it is built for. The first question is the one that matters: the other nine explain your answer.

| # | Question | How to test it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Was it worth my time?** | Answer yes or no after everything else, then say why or why not |
| 2 | **Better than what I'd use instead?** | Name what you would otherwise use for the same thing, and compare |
| 3 | **Did I do what it is for, with no help?** | Pick the app's main goal and try it without asking anyone |
| 4 | **Correct and complete?** | Check one answer, score, or result yourself |
| 5 | **Intuitive?** | Notice your first click: what you expected, and what happened |
| 6 | **Do the visuals help?** | Look for five seconds, then say who it is for and what to do first |
| 7 | **Survives mistakes?** | Reload, submit twice, and type something wrong |
| 8 | **Fast?** | Count the seconds to the first result you care about, and watch each touch get an answer |
| 9 | **Works by keyboard?** | Do the main goal with Tab and Enter only |
| 10 | **Good at its own purpose?** | Judge what it is built for: fun, holding attention, playing together, or a reason to return |

<details>
<summary><strong>Questions to Ask to Gauge Quality</strong></summary>

Use these only if you do not know what to look for. AI now builds a clean, working app in an afternoon, so that earns a 3: these ask about what is left.

- **Worth my time:** Am I glad I spent these minutes, or did it only keep me tapping?
- **Better than the alternative:** Did I learn more, decide better, save effort, or enjoy it more than my usual way?
- **No help:** Did I finish, give up, or only think I finished?
- **Correct:** Could I act on what it told me?
- **Intuitive:** Did I ever have to guess what a thing was for?
- **Visuals:** Did my eye go to the right thing first?
- **Mistakes:** Did it keep my work and tell me what went wrong?
- **Fast:** Did anything I touched do nothing?
- **Keyboard:** Where exactly did I get blocked?
- **Its own purpose:** Did I want another round, could I tell what the other person did, or would I need it again?

</details>
