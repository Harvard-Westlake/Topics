## The Rubric

Use each app for at least three minutes. If it is built for several people, test it with another tester. Then score the class's six criteria from 1 to 5.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Multi-user experience** | Other people change nothing | I can see others, but they do not matter | It is better because someone else is there |
| **Overall enjoyment** | I wanted to stop | Fine while it lasted | I wanted another round when time was up |
| **Sustained attention** | I looked away in the first minute | It held me until it repeated itself | Three minutes passed and I had not noticed |
| **Visuals** | The look hides what to do | Clean, like any app AI makes | The look shows what to do and belongs to this app alone |
| **Reactivity** | Things I touch do nothing | It answers, slowly or plainly | Everything answers at once, to me and to others |
| **How intuitive** | I needed help to start | I worked it out by trial and error | I did the right thing first, with no instructions |

**Is it engaging?** That is the question the six scores add up to: answer it last, yes or no.

Copy this once per app and fill it in:


<details>
<summary><strong>Questions to Ask to Gauge Quality</strong></summary>

Use these only if you do not know what to look for. A year ago, an app that worked and looked clean was impressive. AI now builds that in an afternoon, so it earns a 3: the questions below are about what is left.

- **Multi-user experience:** Did something happen with a second person that could not happen alone? Did I see their action within a second?
- **Overall enjoyment:** When time was up, did I want one more round? Which moment would I show a friend?
- **Sustained attention:** At what second did I first look away, and what was on the screen? How long before it started repeating itself?
- **Visuals:** Could this look belong to any app, or only this one? Does the screen show me what just happened without my reading anything?
- **Reactivity:** Did anything I touched do nothing? Does it react to the other people, or only to me?
- **How intuitive:** How many seconds until something I did worked? Did I ever have to guess what a thing was for?
- **Engaging or addictive:** Am I glad I spent the time, or did it only keep me tapping?

</details>
