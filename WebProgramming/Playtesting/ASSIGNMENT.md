# Assignment - Playtesting

**Lesson:** [Playtesting](README.md)  
**Due:** Before the start of next class

---

## The Draw

Everyone gets a **number**, and the numbers of **four, five, or six classmates** to playtest, picked at random. The class list shows the live link that belongs to each number.

- **Before you test:** your own live link is on the class list next to your number, and it works on a device that is not yours.
- **You are a stranger:** do not ask the builder anything while you test, and do not explain your own app to the people testing it.

---

## Playtest Each App

Use each app on your list for at least three minutes. If it is built for several people at once, test it together with another tester.

Fill in one form for every app. Copy it once per app:

```
App number:                                               
My first ten seconds, before I understood it:             
Most interactive element, and what it did when I used it: 
Best moment, and exactly what was on the screen:          
Where I got stuck or hesitated, and what I tried:         
Something I clicked or typed that did nothing:            
The moment I would have left, and why:                    
One change, as what a user would then do differently:     
```

**Every line names something that happened on the screen.** The words good, bad, fun, boring, and confusing are not answers on their own: say what you did and what the app did.

| Too plain | Specific enough |
|---|---|
| "The best moment was the ending." | "The best moment was my name appearing on the scoreboard next to the other tester's, a second after I finished." |
| "Navigation was bad." | "I pressed Back to change my answer and it threw me out to the first page." |

---

## Success Criteria

- [ ] **Link on the list:** your own app's live link is on the class list and opens on someone else's device.
- [ ] **Every app tested:** you used each app on your list for at least three minutes, as a stranger.
- [ ] **One form per app:** every line of every form is filled in.
- [ ] **Behaviors, not opinions:** each answer says what you did and what the screen did.
- [ ] **Usable alone:** the builder could act on each form without asking you a question.
