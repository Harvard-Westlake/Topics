**Due:** Next class

## Test, Review, and Fix Your Partner's Code

1. **Partner assignment (teacher-chosen)** — check the posted partner list
2. **Collaborator access** — your partner adds you as a **Write** collaborator on their fork (**Settings → Collaborators → Add people → Write**); accept the invite
3. **Run the full testing checklist** from the [lesson](README.md) — functional and behavioral/user
4. **Post the PR write-up** on their pull request, with sections: **Functional**, **Correctness**, **Errors**, **UX**, **UI vs /admin**, **Suggestions** — including repro steps, screenshots, and links to any references you used
5. **Accountability** — call out all non-working functions and poor UX; missed bugs that get merged will reduce your grade for approving them
6. **Get it to "approvable"** — propose or commit fixes (or request changes) until tests pass, errors are gone, and UX/UI issues are addressed; re-test, and when everything meets the checklist, **Approve** the PR

---

## Grading Rubric

| Criterion (weight) | Full credit | Partial / No credit |
|---|---|---|
| **Functional behavior** (25) | Core flows work as stated; no blocking issues. | Key flows missing/broken or unclear behavior. |
| **Correctness vs. spec/real behavior** (20) | Behavior matches expected/official behavior; edge cases verified. | Mismatches with expected behavior; unclear or unverified assumptions. |
| **Errors & stability** (15) | No console/network/server errors during load/use; stable. | Errors present, flakiness, or unhandled failures. |
| **UX quality** (10) | Engaging, responsive, clear feedback/empty/error states. | Clunky, slow, or confusing interactions. No mobile support unless exemption given for complexity. |
| **UI intuitiveness** (10) | Navigation and labels are obvious; flows are self-explanatory. | Hard to discover or understand; misleading labels/flows. |
| **UI parity with `/admin`** (10) | Visual/behavioral match with `/admin` where required. | Noticeable visual/behavioral drift from `/admin`. |
| **PR review write-up & accountability** (10) | Concise sections, repro steps/screens, all issues called out; do not approve with known bugs. | Vague/missing notes; missed bugs or approving with defects. |

---

## Success Criteria

Confirm each of the following before submitting:

- [ ] **Collaborator access granted both ways** — you are on your partner's fork with Write access, and they are on yours
- [ ] **Checklist completed** — you tested functional behavior, correctness, errors, UX, and `/admin` parity
- [ ] **Write-up posted** — your review comment on their PR contains all six sections with repro steps
- [ ] **Issues driven to resolution** — fixes were proposed, committed, or formally requested until the PR met the bar
- [ ] **Approval is honest** — you approved only after the checklist genuinely passed, with no known bugs outstanding
