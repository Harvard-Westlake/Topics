## What to Do

The goal of today is **not** to have the AI build or create anything for you. The goal is for it to **know and understand your tastes**. The more it knows your desires, tastes, preferences, likes, and dislikes, the more it will align with your deepest intentions when it does tasks for you later.

1. Have conversations with the AI (Codex or ChatGPT) so it knows who you are better. Tell it exactly what you find entertaining, engaging, enjoyable, and interesting in web content. If you need a prompt to start:
   ```
   Ask me about what I find enjoyable about web experiences, and the
   mechanisms and features on the web that I enjoy. Pry and discover the
   nuance of what makes my tastes unique, and gather and store a permanent
   recording of my tastes. Discover why my tastes are the way they are and
   what I find genuinely interesting.
   ```
2. When it knows you well, ask it:
   ```
   What do you know about my tastes?
   ```

> **Note:** Do not ask it to build anything until it understands your tastes.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] **Nothing built:** the AI made no pages or code, it only learned about you.
- [ ] **Recognizably you:** we can tell WHO you are from its answer, without any names, because its understanding of your tastes is that complete.
