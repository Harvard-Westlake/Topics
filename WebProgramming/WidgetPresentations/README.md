# Widget Presentations
*<font color="#8b949e">A widget presentation is a short live demo of a classmate's widget, told through what you found when you reviewed it.</font>*

</div>

---

Today there is no new lesson. Every reviewer presents the widget they reviewed: a one-minute walkthrough, then what they tested, what they found, and what got fixed. The class sees every widget, and every review.

## <font color="#388bfd">☑️ Check for Understanding</font>

- [ ] Walk a first-time visitor through a widget, from arriving to the payoff, in one minute.
- [ ] Explain the most important problem your review found and how it was resolved.
- [ ] Name one thing from another group's widget or review that you would use in your own.

## <font color="#388bfd">🚀 Stretch Goals</font>

- [ ] Ask a presenter one question that their review did not answer.
