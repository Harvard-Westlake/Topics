## What to Build

Finish this before the start of the second class period.

- **An approved topic:** you searched for tools that already teach it, can say what yours does differently, and your teacher approved it.
- **A fork:** your own fork of the class project repository (your teacher shares the link).
- **A `PLAN.md`** in your fork, written by you, that answers:
  - what the learner sees first;
  - what the learner can do;
  - how the parts connect;
  - what the widget remembers;
  - why it beats the way you were taught.
- **Committed and pushed** before any AI rewrites or formats it.

**Verify:**

1. Your fork's history shows `PLAN.md` committed before any widget code.
2. Read the first committed version. It is in your own words.
