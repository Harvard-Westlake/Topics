## What to Build

The widget your plan describes, shipped:

- **On a branch** in your fork, in small commits.
- **Every planned part works,** and what it teaches is correct.
- **It remembers:** a learner's progress survives a reload.
- **It looks like learnhw:** the styles match [learnhw.web.app/admin](https://learnhw.web.app/admin).
- **It is live:** deployed on your Firebase site, with a button on your main page that opens it.
- **A pull request** is open, with a description of what the widget teaches.

**Verify:**

1. Open the live widget in a private window, with the console open. Use every part. No errors appear.
2. Make some progress, reload the page, and confirm the progress is still there.
3. Open it on a phone. It is usable.
4. Your pull request shows the branch's commits and the description.
