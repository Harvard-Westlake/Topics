# Final Wrap-Up and Deployment
*<font color="#8b949e">Close out every pull request, deploy the latest version, and ship a product that works</font>*

<font color="#e3b341">Review</font>

</div>

---

Today's goal: finalize your project, close out pull requests, make any final adjustments, and deploy your latest version to Firebase. Your app must be **usable and functional by the end of class**, or credit will be heavily penalized.

This is the last day of the project. Everything you have practiced this unit — issues, branches, reviews, merges, documentation — converges on one deliverable: a live, working product.

---

## <font color="#388bfd">Step 1 — Close pull requests</font>

Review **all open pull requests** on your repository, including the ones classmates sent you in [Help a Classmate](../HelpAClassmate/):

- **Merge or decline** each one based on its relevance to your project
- Make sure your repository reflects the most recent and correct version of your app

> **Tip:**
> Check the history of your MVP.md file — every contributor updated it, so its history is a quick map of what was added and by whom.

---

## <font color="#388bfd">Step 2 — Final code adjustments (15 minutes)</font>

Focus on:

1. Fixing bugs or errors that prevent usability
2. Adding any final touches to ensure a polished user experience
3. Ensuring all critical functionality works as intended — menus, gameplay mechanics, interactions

> **Warning:**
> Avoid major rewrites. Time is limited — a working product with rough edges beats a broken product with ambitious changes.

---

## <font color="#388bfd">Step 3 — Deploy to Firebase (10 minutes)</font>

1. Run the deploy:

```bash
firebase deploy
```

2. Test the **live** version of your app:
   - Does it load without errors?
   - Is it functional and playable?
3. If any issues arise, fix them immediately and redeploy

---

## <font color="#388bfd">Step 4 — Submit for review</font>

Before leaving class, submit the link to your live Firebase app — even though you have submitted it before, this submission marks the final version.

Key notes:

- **Usability matters** — test thoroughly
- Projects must be **live and functional** before next class
- This is your **final product**
